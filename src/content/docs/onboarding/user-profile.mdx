# Your Profile

##### Manage your identity and DAO visibility on Nouns Builder

Your profile page is a participation dashboard for your connected wallet. It brings together your identity, links, delegation controls, DAO memberships, activity, and token holdings. Open it by connecting your wallet and selecting your profile from the avatar menu in the top-right corner of the app.

## Profile Overview

Once connected, your profile displays:

- Your **ENS name** (or shortened wallet address if no ENS is set)
- Your **wallet address**, with a one-click copy button
- Your profile links, when configured
- Summary cards for your DAOs, tokens, votes, proposals and bids
- An **Activity** feed of your recent on-chain activity across DAOs
- A **Tokens** section listing the DAO tokens you hold

## DAOs and Activity

The **DAOs** section lists every DAO in which your connected wallet holds a token. Each card shows the DAO name, logo and network. Select a DAO card to filter the Activity and Tokens sections to that DAO; select it again to return to the combined view.

Use the Activity menu to filter the feed by event type, including bids, wins, settles, proposal creation, votes, proposal updates and executions.

## Editing Your DAO List

You can customize which DAOs are visible on your profile and in your wallet dropdown by using **Edit** mode.

<Steps>

1. **Open Edit mode**

   The page starts in browse mode. Click the **Edit** button next to the DAOs heading to begin changing the list.

   <img
     src="/guides/profileGuide/daos.png"
     alt="Profile DAOs section before editing"
     width="400"
   />

2. **Use the edit controls**

   Click **Edit** to reveal the controls for changing the DAO list.

   <img
     src="/guides/profileGuide/daos-edit.png"
     alt="Profile DAOs section in Edit mode with remove and reorder controls"
     width="400"
   />

3. **Hide a DAO**

   Click the **minus (−)** icon next to a DAO to remove it from the visible list and wallet dropdown.

4. **Reorder your DAOs**

   Click and drag the **move** handle on the right side of a DAO entry to reorder how DAOs appear in your list.

5. **Save your changes**

   Click **Done** to exit Edit mode and save your preferences.

</Steps>

## Tokens

The Tokens section is collapsed by default so the profile stays easy to scan. Select the chevron beside **Tokens** to expand the gallery, then use the sort menu to change the order.

![Collapsed Tokens section on the profile](/guides/profileGuide/tokens-collapsed.png)

![Expanded token gallery on the profile](/guides/profileGuide/tokens-expanded.png)

Selecting a DAO card filters the token gallery to that DAO. Token cards show the owned NFTs and their collection context.

:::note

DAOs and tokens are only shown when the connected wallet has the relevant on-chain ownership. If a DAO or token you expect to see is missing, verify that you are connected with the correct wallet and network.

:::

## Wallet and Delegation Actions

The profile and wallet menus provide quick access to your wallet address, profile URL, block explorers and profile links. You can also delegate to a profile when delegation is available for the selected DAO.

When viewing your own profile, select an owned NFT to start the transfer flow. Choose a recipient, review the warning and transaction details, then submit the transfer from your wallet. Token transfers are limited to one chain at a time.
